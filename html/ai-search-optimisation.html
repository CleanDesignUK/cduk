FAQs

Questions about ai search optimisation · aeo · geo.

Useful answers before you decide whether this service fits your business.

What is GEO?

GEO usually means Generative Engine Optimisation: improving the chance that a brand or page is retrieved and referenced in generative search experiences.

What is AEO?

AEO usually means Answer Engine Optimisation: structuring useful content so answer systems can understand and surface it for specific questions.

Do I need an llms.txt file for Google?

Google says no. Its 2026 guidance states that special AI text files are not required for Google Search generative features. We focus first on content, technical SEO and real business signals.

Can AI-search visibility be measured?

It can be monitored through a mix of Search Console generative-AI reporting, analytics referrals, brand/entity monitoring and query testing, while recognising that no third-party tool can expose an AI system’s internal ranking logic.
