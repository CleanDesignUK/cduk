Shopify vs WooCommerce for UK Small Businesses in 2026

Shopify vs WooCommerce for UK ecommerce businesses: compare ownership, maintenance, flexibility, apps, SEO, content management and operating needs.

Published 12 October 2026 9 min read

The best ecommerce platform is the one your business can operate reliably. Shopify and WooCommerce can both support serious stores, but they make different trade-offs around hosting, maintenance, control and ecosystem.

When Shopify is a strong fit

Shopify is hosted, which removes much of the server and update responsibility. It is often attractive to teams that want a predictable commerce admin, mature app marketplace and fewer infrastructure decisions.

When WooCommerce is a strong fit

WooCommerce runs on WordPress and gives the business more control over hosting, content structure and extensions. That flexibility is valuable, but it also creates more responsibility for maintenance, security, compatibility and performance.

What about SEO?

Both can rank. The practical difference is how the chosen theme, apps/plugins, content architecture and technical setup are handled. Strong category pages, useful product information, internal linking, performance and index control matter more than the platform logo.

Compare total operating cost, not just plan price

Platform fees are only one part of ecommerce cost. Include themes, apps/plugins, payment fees, developer support, product content, photography, integrations and staff time. A cheaper platform can become more expensive if it requires constant technical attention.

Use your operational needs to decide

If the team wants a hosted commerce-first system, Shopify may be easier. If the business has content-heavy WordPress needs or requires deeper control, WooCommerce may make more sense. Map the workflows first, then choose the platform.
