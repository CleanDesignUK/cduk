FAQs

Questions about brand identity & visual systems.

Useful answers before you decide whether this service fits your business.

Do you offer logo design only?

We can help with focused logo work, but the strongest outcome usually comes from treating the logo as part of a wider identity system.

Can you refresh an existing brand without replacing everything?

Yes. A brand refresh can retain recognisable elements while improving typography, colour, consistency and digital usability.

Can the branding be used on my website and social media?

Yes. We design with practical digital use in mind so the identity can carry consistently across the website, social profiles and everyday marketing.
