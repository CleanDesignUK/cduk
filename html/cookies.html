Cookie policy

A cookie banner that actually controls analytics.

Non-essential analytics is not loaded until you actively allow it.

Short version: Clean Design UK uses local browser storage to remember your cookie preference. Google Analytics is optional and only loads after you click “Allow analytics”.

Essential preference storage

The site stores your cookie choice in localStorage under cduk_cookie_consent_v2 . This prevents the banner appearing on every page and records whether analytics was accepted or rejected.

Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics using measurement ID G-TFED1HQFDE. Google Analytics may then set cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browser sessions and measure site usage. If you reject analytics before it loads, those analytics scripts are not loaded by this website.

Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer at any time. If you change from accepted to rejected during the same browser session, the site sends a denied analytics consent update and attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies for cleandesignuk.com.

Why we ask first

ICO guidance requires a valid consent mechanism for non-essential analytics/storage technologies. The site therefore does not treat continued browsing as consent.

Form submissions

Web3Forms is contacted when you intentionally submit an enquiry form. The site does not load a visible CAPTCHA or require you to tick an anti-spam checkbox.
