MORE THAN A LANDING PAGE

01 / INTRODUCTION

A good sales funnel doesn't just collect enquiries. It helps you act on them.

A sales funnel is the journey a potential customer takes from discovering your business to making an enquiry, booking, purchasing or becoming a customer. It brings together relevant pages, clear offers, contact forms, follow-up messages and the next steps in your sales process.

Marketing automation uses software and rules to carry out suitable repeatable tasks, such as routing a new lead into a CRM, sending a requested confirmation, assigning a follow-up task or updating a sales pipeline.

When the two work together, your website becomes part of an organised customer journey rather than a standalone contact form.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Sales funnels. Automation. Clear answers.

Common questions about sales funnel design, CRM automation and marketing workflows for businesses in North Wales and across the United Kingdom.

Ask about your project
What is a sales funnel?

A sales funnel is the series of steps a prospective customer follows from discovering a business to taking an intended action, such as requesting a quotation, booking a consultation or making a purchase. A funnel can include advertising, landing pages, forms, confirmations and sales follow-up.

What is marketing automation?

Marketing automation uses software and predefined rules to carry out suitable repetitive tasks. Examples include adding a form enquiry to a CRM, sending a requested confirmation, assigning a sales task and scheduling an appropriate follow-up message. Good automation still requires accurate data, testing and oversight.

What sales funnel services do you offer in North Wales?

Clean Design UK offers sales funnel planning and design, landing pages, enquiry capture, CRM pipeline setup, follow-up workflows, booking journeys and GoHighLevel support. We work with businesses in Anglesey, Bangor, Gwynedd, Conwy, Wrexham and across North Wales.

Do you offer sales funnel design across the UK?

Yes. Clean Design UK supports businesses throughout Wales, England and the wider United Kingdom. Projects can be planned and delivered remotely, including funnel design, CRM workflows and automation configuration.

Can you connect a sales funnel to my existing website?

Often, yes. A funnel may be built into an existing website or use dedicated landing pages. The approach depends on the website platform, hosting, form capabilities and available CRM integrations. We can review the setup before recommending a solution.

Can you automate leads from website contact forms?

Where supported by the form provider and CRM, new enquiries can be sent to contact records or pipeline stages, trigger notifications and create follow-up tasks. We also consider data quality, duplicate records and error handling.

Can you automate email, SMS and WhatsApp messages?

Email, SMS and WhatsApp workflows may be possible depending on the platforms, messaging permissions and provider rules. Marketing messages must comply with applicable UK privacy and electronic marketing requirements, including UK GDPR and PECR where relevant. We do not recommend unsolicited mass messaging.

What is CRM automation?

CRM automation uses rules and integrations to maintain contact records, move leads between appropriate pipeline stages, assign tasks and coordinate follow-ups. It helps businesses organise sales activity, but needs clear processes and human review for exceptions.

Do you build sales funnels using GoHighLevel?

Yes. Clean Design UK provides GoHighLevel support covering funnel-related setup, forms, customer records, sales pipelines and workflow configuration. Available features depend on your account plan and settings.

Can a sales funnel help a small business generate enquiries?

A sales funnel can help a small business give visitors a clearer route to enquire or book. For example, a tradesperson in Anglesey may use a service page, quotation form and organised follow-up workflow. Results depend on demand, the offer, website quality and marketing.

Do you provide automation for corporate and B2B companies?

Yes. We can discuss B2B lead capture, enquiry qualification, CRM pipelines, team assignment and appointment workflows. More complex projects may require additional planning around approvals, integrations, permissions and existing systems.

Can you automate bookings and appointment reminders?

Depending on your booking platform, suitable workflows can send confirmations, manage reminders, notify staff and update CRM records. Integration and messaging capabilities must be checked for the specific tools being used.

How much does a sales funnel cost in the UK?

Sales funnel pricing depends on the number of pages, content requirements, CRM setup, automation complexity and integrations. A simple enquiry funnel differs from a multi-stage sales process used by a larger business. Clean Design UK can provide a quotation once the scope has been discussed.

How much does marketing automation cost?

Automation costs can include planning and setup, software subscriptions, messaging charges, integration services and ongoing maintenance. The final cost depends on the platforms and workflow requirements.

Can automation replace our sales team?

Automation is most useful for repeatable tasks and clear process steps. It does not replace the judgement, care and relationship-building needed for many sales conversations. We favour workflows that support people and provide sensible handover points.

What is the difference between a website and a sales funnel?

A website usually provides broader information about a business, its services and how to contact it. A sales funnel focuses on guiding a particular audience towards a specific action and the steps that follow. Both can work together.

How long does it take to build a sales funnel and automation?

Timescales depend on the number of pages, content availability, CRM requirements, integrations, approvals and testing. We would assess the scope and agree a delivery plan before starting work.

Will a sales funnel guarantee more sales?

No sales funnel or automation system can guarantee sales. Results depend on factors such as traffic, the offer, pricing, audience, market demand and follow-up quality. A well-designed process can help make the customer journey clearer and easier to manage.

Can you improve an existing sales funnel or CRM?

Yes. We can review existing landing pages, enquiry forms, CRM stages and automation workflows to identify usability issues, unnecessary steps or opportunities to improve the process.

Can funnels and automation support SEO and digital marketing?

Yes. SEO, Google Ads and social media marketing can help people discover a business, while a funnel provides a relevant next step. Connected lead handling can also help businesses assess the quality of resulting enquiries.
