Website Design Advice

Planning Your Business Website

Important considerations before commissioning a new website or redesigning an existing one.

What Pages Does a Business Website Need?

Most websites benefit from clear homepage information, individual service pages, company details, examples of work and a contact page. The exact structure depends on your business and customers.

Template or Bespoke Website Design?

Templates can work well for straightforward projects, while bespoke designs allow more flexibility in layout, functionality and content structure. The right choice depends on the project's requirements.

How Can a Website Generate Enquiries?

Customers need clear service details, relevant information and easy contact options. Useful landing pages, quotations forms and visible calls to action can support the enquiry process.

Website Design FAQs

Answers about website design costs, development, responsive websites, SEO, redesigns and services across North Wales and the UK.

Ask about your website
Do you provide website design in North Wales?

Yes. Clean Design UK offers professional website design, responsive development and website redesign services across North Wales, including Anglesey, Bangor, Caernarfon, Gwynedd, Conwy, Llandudno, Wrexham and surrounding areas. We also serve businesses throughout Wales and the United Kingdom.

How much does a professional business website cost?

The cost depends on the number of pages, design requirements, content, functionality, integrations and project complexity. A straightforward service website usually requires less development than a large ecommerce or booking website. We provide quotations based on your specific requirements.

How long does it take to design a website?

Timescales depend on the project scope, availability of content and photographs, feedback stages and technical requirements. We discuss realistic timescales during planning and agree project milestones where appropriate.

Will my website work on smartphones and tablets?

Yes. Responsive website design is an important part of our development process. We consider desktop, tablet and mobile layouts, including readable typography, appropriate navigation, touch controls and usable forms.

Do your website designs include SEO?

Depending on the agreed scope, we can include foundational SEO work such as logical page structure, headings, titles, meta descriptions, internal linking, image optimisation, canonical URLs and suitable structured data. Ongoing SEO services and content development can be discussed separately.

Can you redesign my website without losing SEO?

We can plan a redesign with existing search visibility in mind, reviewing useful pages, valuable content, current URLs and redirects where required. Search rankings can fluctuate during website migrations, so it is not possible to guarantee that rankings will remain unchanged.

Do you create websites for local tradespeople?

Yes. We design websites for construction businesses, tradespeople, professional service providers and other local companies. These can feature individual service pages, work galleries, quotation forms and genuine service-area information.

Can website design improve local Google visibility?

Search-friendly website design can support local visibility through clear service descriptions, business information, relevant location content and appropriate technical SEO. Google Business Profile information and other local factors also influence local search results. Specific rankings cannot be guaranteed.

What is the difference between web design and web development?

Web design focuses on layout, appearance, navigation and user experience. Web development involves implementing the website's structure, code and functionality. Business websites generally need both to create a finished, functioning website.

Can you add contact forms, booking systems or CRM integrations?

Yes. Depending on the project's requirements, we can integrate enquiry forms, booking systems, quotation requests and suitable third-party services. We also offer CRM and automation support, including GoHighLevel services.

Can you help with website content?

Yes. We can help plan website content, service page structure, headings, business information and relevant location-specific content. Accurate, useful and original information benefits both visitors and search engines.

What are AEO and GEO in website design?

Answer Engine Optimisation, or AEO, considers how to present useful answers to common customer questions. Generative Engine Optimisation, or GEO, considers how content may be understood and referenced by AI-assisted search systems. Clear explanations, accessible pages, reliable information and established SEO practices can support machine-readable content. Neither approach guarantees inclusion in AI-generated answers.

Do you offer website design throughout the UK?

Yes. We serve businesses across North Wales, Wales and the wider United Kingdom. Suitable website projects can be managed remotely through online discussions, design reviews and agreed project stages.

What do I need before starting a website project?

It helps to know your main services, target customers, desired website features and any existing branding or website content. If you already have a website, share its URL. Remaining requirements can be discussed during the initial consultation.
