FAQs

Questions about website design & development.

Useful answers before you decide whether this service fits your business.

How much does a business website cost?

Website cost depends on scope, page count, functionality, content, integrations and the amount of strategy required. We quote against the actual project rather than forcing very different businesses into one package.

Will my website be SEO friendly?

Yes. We build the technical and structural foundations: crawlable pages, sensible headings, metadata, canonicals, internal links, mobile performance and structured data where it is useful.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign can keep useful content and brand equity while improving architecture, mobile UX, speed, conversion and search visibility.

Which platforms do you work with?

Platform choice depends on the project. We can advise on a maintainable option based on editing needs, ecommerce requirements, integrations and budget.
