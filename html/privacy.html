Information submitted through forms

When you submit an enquiry, the form asks for your name, email, phone number and the service you are interested in. The form is processed through Web3Forms so that the enquiry can be delivered to us. We use this information to respond to your request, discuss services and keep a reasonable business record of the enquiry.

Analytics

Google Analytics is optional on this website. It is not loaded until you choose “Allow analytics” in the cookie banner. If you reject analytics, the site remains fully usable without Google Analytics loading.

Spam prevention

Forms use a hidden honeypot field, basic client-side validation and local submission throttling to reduce automated junk. These controls do not require a visible CAPTCHA checkbox.

Third-party services

The site may connect to Web3Forms when you submit an enquiry, Google Analytics when you have consented, and Google Fonts to load the chosen typography. Links to social platforms and WhatsApp only connect you to those services when you use them.
