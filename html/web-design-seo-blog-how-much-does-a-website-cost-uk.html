How Much Does a Website Cost in the UK? What Actually Changes the Quote

Understand what changes the cost of a UK business website: strategy, page count, content, ecommerce, integrations, SEO, design complexity and ongoing support.

Published 12 October 2026 8 min read

There is no single useful answer to “how much does a website cost?” because a five-page service website and a large ecommerce build are different products. A better approach is to understand the variables that change the work, the risk and the long-term cost of ownership.

Page count is only part of scope

Ten simple pages can be easier than one complex quoting tool. Cost is shaped by the number of unique layouts, decision logic, integrations, content requirements and the amount of discovery needed before design starts.

Content quality changes the project

A site can only be as clear as the information going into it. If the project includes keyword research, content architecture, copy structure, photography or case-study development, that work should be part of the quote rather than hidden inside “design”.

Ecommerce adds operational complexity

Products, variants, shipping, tax, payments, account areas, stock, email flows and returns all add decisions. The platform subscription is rarely the largest ecommerce cost over the life of the store.

SEO changes more than metadata

A search-led build may require keyword clustering, redirects, content migration, location strategy, schema, internal linking and Search Console setup. That is different from adding a title tag after the design is finished.

Compare the ongoing cost as well

Ask what happens after launch: hosting, software licences, maintenance, backups, content changes, support and ownership. A low initial price can be poor value if the site is difficult to edit or depends on expensive tools you do not need.

How to get a more accurate quote

Prepare your main services, target customers, must-have functions, examples you like, current website problems and preferred launch window. A good provider should be able to explain which items materially affect the quote and which are optional.
