WHAT IS GOHIGHLEVEL?

01 / THE PLATFORM

What Is GoHighLevel and What Can It Do?

GoHighLevel (GHL) is a customer relationship management and marketing platform that brings together the tools businesses use to attract leads, manage customers, book appointments and automate repeatable tasks.

Instead of managing separate systems for landing pages, contact forms, sales pipelines, booking calendars and follow-up campaigns, businesses can manage many of these functions in one platform. The best setup depends on what your business actually needs.

One platform. Multiple business functions. CRM · Websites · Funnels · Ecommerce · Automation

GOHIGHLEVEL CRM FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GoHighLevel CRM Setup, Automation & Support FAQs

Clear answers about HighLevel websites, sales funnels, ecommerce, CRM, email automation, calendar bookings and support in North Wales and the UK.

What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel, also called HighLevel or GHL, is a CRM and marketing platform that combines contact management, sales pipelines, websites, landing pages, funnels, automation workflows, email marketing, calendars and other business tools.

Does Clean Design UK provide GoHighLevel setup in North Wales?

Yes. Clean Design UK provides independent GoHighLevel CRM setup, funnel design, workflow automation and support for businesses in Anglesey, Bangor, Gwynedd, Conwy, Wrexham and throughout North Wales.

Can you build a website in GoHighLevel?

Yes. Clean Design UK can design and configure GoHighLevel websites and landing pages, including mobile-friendly layouts, enquiry forms, calls to action and supported CRM integrations. We can also discuss whether a separate website platform is more appropriate.

Can GoHighLevel run an ecommerce store?

Yes. HighLevel has an ecommerce store builder that supports product listings, cart and checkout pages, payment-provider integrations and order management. Suitability depends on the products, fulfilment requirements and functionality needed.

Can GoHighLevel automate email follow-ups?

Yes. Supported workflows can respond to events such as form submissions and appointment bookings with appropriate email messages, CRM updates, notifications and follow-up tasks. Email sending requires suitable domain setup and applicable permissions.

Can GoHighLevel manage appointment bookings?

Yes. GoHighLevel supports booking calendars and appointment-related workflows. Businesses can use these for consultation requests, service appointments, team scheduling and suitable confirmations or reminders.

Can you fix an existing GoHighLevel account?

Clean Design UK can review an existing GoHighLevel setup, investigate forms, funnels, calendars, pipelines and workflow configurations, and propose changes based on the access and features available.

Is GoHighLevel suitable for small businesses?

GoHighLevel can suit small businesses that need lead management, booking, marketing communications or automation. Whether it is cost-effective depends on the business's requirements, subscription costs and current software.

How much does GoHighLevel CRM setup cost in the UK?

The cost of professional GHL setup depends on the number of funnels or websites, CRM pipelines, booking calendars, integrations and workflows required. Software subscription fees and provider charges are separate unless explicitly included in your quotation.

Can you help businesses outside North Wales?

Yes. We offer remote GoHighLevel setup and support for businesses across Wales, England, Scotland and the rest of the UK. Projects can be planned and delivered online.

Can you connect my existing website to GoHighLevel?

In many cases, yes. Existing websites can use supported GHL forms, calendar tools, integrations or API-based connections. The best approach depends on your current website platform and technical setup.

Can GoHighLevel connect with other software?

HighLevel offers native integrations, API functionality and workflow actions that can connect with other systems. The available connections depend on your account permissions, plan and the third-party software involved.

Can you set up SMS and WhatsApp automation?

Depending on the account configuration, messaging providers and available integrations, these workflows may be possible. Automated customer messages must comply with applicable data protection and electronic marketing requirements, including UK GDPR and PECR where relevant.

Do I need a GoHighLevel subscription?

You will need an appropriate HighLevel account or access arrangement to use its hosted CRM and platform features. There may also be separate costs for domains, email, messaging, payment processing and other services.

Can GoHighLevel help with sales lead management?

Yes. GHL pipelines can organise new enquiries, lead stages, appointments and follow-up tasks. Automation can support consistent administration, but cannot guarantee new leads or sales.

How long does a GoHighLevel setup take?

Timescales vary with the number of pages, workflows, calendars and integrations required. An existing account review may be smaller than a full website, ecommerce and CRM implementation. We can discuss delivery stages after reviewing your requirements.

Can you migrate contacts into GoHighLevel?

Contact imports may be possible depending on the quality and format of the source data. Before importing or communicating with contacts, the business must consider lawful data handling, existing permissions and relevant privacy obligations.

Is Clean Design UK officially affiliated with GoHighLevel?

Clean Design UK provides independent GoHighLevel setup and support services. References to GoHighLevel describe the software platform and do not imply an official partnership, endorsement or certification.
