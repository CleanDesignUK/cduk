SEO · AEO · GEO

Search is changing. The fundamentals still matter.

Google Search, AI Overviews and conversational search experiences can present information differently. Your website still needs accurate, original, useful and accessible content.

SEO

Search engine optimisation improves technical accessibility and relevance for organic search.

AEO

Answer engine optimisation focuses on clearly addressing questions and making answers easy to understand.

GEO

Generative engine optimisation refers to improving how content can be understood and considered by AI-powered discovery systems.

These are overlapping practices, not three entirely separate ranking systems. There is no special markup that guarantees a mention in an AI-generated response.

Common questions from businesses considering SEO services in North Wales, Wales and across the United Kingdom.

Ask about your website
What does an SEO agency in North Wales do?

An SEO agency helps businesses improve the relevance, technical accessibility and quality of their website for organic search. Clean Design UK's SEO services can include audits, technical fixes, content planning, on-page SEO, local SEO and ongoing search performance reviews for businesses across North Wales and the UK.

Do you provide SEO services across Anglesey and Bangor?

Yes. Clean Design UK works with businesses in Anglesey, Bangor, Caernarfon, Holyhead, Gwynedd and other parts of North Wales. SEO work can address local service searches, website content, Google Business Profile information and technical issues relevant to each business.

Can you work with businesses outside North Wales?

Yes. Although Clean Design UK has an address in Caergeiliog, Anglesey, our SEO services are available to businesses across Wales, England and the wider United Kingdom. Projects can be discussed and delivered remotely.

What is included in an SEO audit?

An SEO audit reviews the website's technical accessibility, indexation, page structure, content relevance, internal links and available search performance data. The exact scope depends on the website, with findings prioritised into practical recommendations.

What is local SEO and why does it matter?

Local SEO helps businesses improve their relevance for searches connected to a particular area. It may involve Google Business Profile information, relevant service-area content, accurate business details and a clear website. It is useful for businesses serving customers in places such as Anglesey, Bangor, Conwy and Wrexham.

Can you improve our Google Business Profile?

Yes. We can review the accuracy and completeness of business details, suitable categories, service descriptions, photographs and relevant website connections. Google determines local results using factors including relevance, distance and prominence, so a particular Maps position cannot be guaranteed.

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO concerns the website features that affect crawling, indexing and user experience. Examples include canonical URLs, redirects, sitemaps, structured data, internal links, page performance and mobile accessibility.

How much do SEO services cost in the UK?

SEO pricing depends on website size, current condition, competition, the number of services or locations involved and whether the project is a one-off audit or ongoing support. Clean Design UK can discuss your requirements and provide a quotation for the agreed scope.

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO results vary by website, competition and the changes made. Technical corrections may be detected after search engines revisit pages, while stronger organic visibility from content and authority development often takes several months or longer. No fixed timeframe or ranking position can be guaranteed.

Can an SEO agency guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No responsible SEO agency can guarantee a specific organic ranking. Search results vary by query, location and Google's systems. Our focus is on useful improvements, measurable search visibility and relevant customer enquiries.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO refers to search engine optimisation. AEO commonly means answer engine optimisation, focused on clear, helpful responses to questions. GEO commonly means generative engine optimisation, which concerns visibility in AI-assisted discovery. The practices overlap, including technically accessible content, accurate information, relevant sources and useful website structure.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google AI will recommend us?

No. AI-generated responses and citations are controlled by the respective systems and may change between searches. We can improve the clarity, accessibility and usefulness of your online information, but no agency can guarantee inclusion or recommendations in AI answers.

Does SEO help small businesses and tradespeople?

SEO can be useful for tradespeople and small businesses when customers search online for their services. A focused strategy may prioritise important service pages, local search relevance, business information and enquiry forms rather than producing large amounts of unnecessary content.

Can you optimise Shopify and ecommerce websites?

Ecommerce SEO can include collection-page structure, product titles and descriptions, duplicate URL checks, technical accessibility and search-friendly product information. The work depends on the ecommerce platform and the organisation of the online shop.

Is SEO better than Google Ads?

SEO and Google Ads serve different purposes. SEO focuses on unpaid organic search relevance and visibility over time. Google Ads provides paid advertising placements while campaigns are active. Some businesses benefit from both, depending on their goals, budget and competition.

Do you provide one-off SEO audits or ongoing SEO?

We can discuss a one-off SEO audit, implementation of specific improvements or an ongoing search visibility project. The appropriate scope depends on your website, business priorities and available resources.
