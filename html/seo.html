FAQs

Questions about seo & search visibility.

Useful answers before you decide whether this service fits your business.

Is SEO still relevant when people use AI search?

Yes. Google’s own guidance says foundational SEO remains relevant to generative AI features because those systems still depend on crawlable, indexable, useful web content.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves discoverability in search. AEO is commonly used for answer-engine visibility and GEO for generative-engine visibility. In practice, the strongest work overlaps: clear entities, helpful content, technical accessibility and authority.

Do you offer local SEO in North Wales?

Yes. Local work can include Google Business Profile alignment, location relevance, service-area content, internal links, citations and conversion improvements.

Can you guarantee first place rankings?

No responsible provider can guarantee a specific organic position. We focus on improving technical quality, relevance, visibility and conversion while measuring progress honestly.
