What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of improving a business's visibility for geographically relevant searches. It includes useful website pages, accurate business information, appropriate local content, and, for eligible businesses, Google Business Profile management.

A business serving North Wales does not necessarily need to attract visitors from every part of the country. A local electrician may primarily want enquiries from Anglesey and Gwynedd, while a professional services company may want customers in Bangor, Conwy and Wrexham.

Local SEO helps search engines understand what the business provides and which locations are genuinely relevant. It works best when the website is useful to visitors, rather than written mainly to repeat keywords.

1. Create Clear, Useful Service Pages

Each significant service should have enough information for a customer to make an informed decision. A roofing company might describe roof repairs, roof replacements and guttering as distinct services if it genuinely offers them.

A helpful service page can explain what is included, who the service is for, how the process works, common customer questions and how to request a quotation.

The page title and main heading should clearly identify the service. Write naturally and avoid placing the same search phrase into every sentence.

2. Describe Your Genuine Service Areas

If your business works across Anglesey, Bangor, Caernarfon and nearby towns, explain this accurately. Customers should know whether you can provide the service in their area.

A specific location page may be useful when you have something meaningful to say about that location, such as locally relevant services, genuine project examples, coverage arrangements or practical contact information.

Creating many pages that only swap one town name for another can produce repetitive content with little value to visitors.

Local SEO works best when your website explains real services and real locations, not when it tries to manufacture relevance.

3. Check Your Website's Technical Foundations

Search engines need to be able to access and understand the important pages of your website. Technical problems can make that more difficult.

  • Use clear, descriptive URLs.
  • Give important pages unique titles and headings.
  • Link related service and location pages together.
  • Ensure pages work on mobile devices.
  • Check that important content is not accidentally blocked from indexing.
  • Use appropriate canonical URLs.
  • Keep unnecessary scripts and oversized images under control.

Structured data can help describe information such as your organisation and services. It should reflect the visible content and real business details. Adding schema markup alone does not guarantee higher rankings or special search features.

4. Keep Business Details Consistent

Your name, contact details and relevant business information should be accurate across your website and important business profiles.

If you are eligible for Google Business Profile, keep the profile updated with your actual business categories, hours, services and customer-facing details.

You can read our separate article on improving your Google Business Profile for a more detailed explanation.

5. Track Relevant Search Traffic and Enquiries

Search impressions and visits can be useful indicators, but the business outcome matters too. Track relevant enquiries, telephone contacts, bookings or other actions where appropriate.

Google Search Console can help you understand how your website appears in Google Search. Website analytics can provide additional information about visitor behaviour, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Local SEO improvements can take time, and results depend on factors including your market, competition and the quality of your website. No responsible agency should promise a particular position on Google.

Local SEO Starting Checklist

  • Identify the core services customers search for.
  • Explain each important service clearly.
  • Describe genuine location coverage.
  • Review Google Business Profile information if eligible.
  • Check mobile usability and website navigation.
  • Review page titles and internal links.
  • Measure enquiries as well as search traffic.

Local SEO FAQs

Is local SEO suitable for small businesses in Anglesey?

Yes. Local SEO can be useful for businesses serving customers in specific areas, including trades, professional services, shops and local education providers.

Can I target Bangor and Gwynedd on the same website?

Yes, where your business genuinely serves both areas. Your site should explain the coverage naturally and provide useful location-specific information when relevant.

How long does local SEO take to work?

There is no guaranteed timeframe. Progress varies depending on the website, market competition, existing visibility and the improvements being made.
