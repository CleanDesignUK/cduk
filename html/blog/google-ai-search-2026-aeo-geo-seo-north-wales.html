What did Google announce about AI Search in 2026?

On 3 June 2026, Google announced additional opportunities, controls and insights for website owners as AI Overviews and AI Mode became part of more searches. The announcement described a Search Console control that lets site owners decide whether their content may be used in Google's generative AI Search features.

Google said the opt-out control does not influence ranking in ordinary Search results outside those AI features. Its August 31 update stated that the features had been rolled out worldwide. Google also described expanded Search Console insights related to appearances in AI experiences. Website owners should check their own Search Console account for the specific data currently available.

This matters because a website may now be discovered through traditional search results, local map results and AI-assisted answers. Each experience can encourage a different type of customer journey.

SEO, AEO and GEO: what is the difference?

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) improves the technical accessibility, relevance and usefulness of a website for conventional search engines. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) is often used to describe writing clear, trustworthy answers that can be found in search and answer experiences. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is a term commonly used for approaches intended to help AI systems understand or refer to accurate business information.

These labels overlap, and none creates a guaranteed route into AI answers. Google's own advice centres on unique content, sound website organisation, helpful page experiences and good-quality visual information. A new AEO plugin cannot replace credible service explanations or a technically accessible website.

A practical six-point AI Search visibility checklist

  1. Explain the service early. Put a specific, useful summary near the top of each main service page.
  2. Answer questions directly. Include a genuinely helpful FAQ where it supports the customer journey.
  3. Show evidence. Publish real project examples, useful product information and customer-facing details.
  4. Use proper structure. Clear heading levels, descriptive image alt text, consistent navigation and valid schema help machines interpret the page.
  5. Keep local information consistent. Your contact details, business coverage and Google Business Profile should reflect reality.
  6. Measure, then improve. Check indexing, enquiries and meaningful landing-page engagement rather than chasing unverified AI citation claims.

For an online shop, add detailed product information and helpful delivery policies. For a tutor, publish age and subject coverage in plain English. For a tradesperson, make it easy to find services, past work and contact options.

Should you turn off inclusion in Google AI Search?

That is a business decision. Google's 2026 announcement describes a way to control whether a website appears in and grounds generative AI Search experiences. An opt-out could reduce appearances and visits from those features, so it is not something to enable automatically because of a headline.

Review what the new settings actually do, the kind of content you publish and whether AI-assisted discovery benefits your business. You can revisit your choice as Search Console reporting and search behaviour evolve. Avoid claims that AI citations or first-page results are guaranteed.
