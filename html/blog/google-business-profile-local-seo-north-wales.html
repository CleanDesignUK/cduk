How do you improve a Google Business Profile?

Verify your eligible business profile, keep your business details accurate, select appropriate categories, describe your services, upload helpful photos and respond to genuine reviews. Support your profile with a website that clearly explains what you do and where you work.

When somebody searches for a local business, Google may show a map, individual business profiles and organic website results. For a small business serving Holyhead, Bangor, Caernarfon or Llandudno, appearing with accurate information can make it easier for prospective customers to get in touch.

Google Business Profile is a free tool for eligible businesses to manage how their information appears in Google Search and Maps. A strong profile is not simply about adding keywords. It is about making your actual business information complete, useful and trustworthy.

1. Complete Your Business Information

Start by reviewing the essentials: business name, phone number, website link, opening hours, location and services. Use the real business name customers know rather than adding extra towns or service keywords to it.

Opening hours matter because customers use them to decide whether to call or visit. Check bank holidays and temporary changes, not just normal weekday hours.

If your business visits customers rather than welcoming them at a staffed premises, review Google's service-area business requirements. A business operating from a private home should not automatically display that address to the public.

2. Choose Accurate Categories and Services

Your primary category should represent your main business activity. Additional categories should describe other genuine parts of your business, not unrelated services you would like to rank for.

A plastering company serving Anglesey and Gwynedd, for example, should explain the plastering and rendering services it actually provides. A tutoring company in Bangor should explain its subjects, age groups and lesson formats rather than relying on a generic business description.

Google Business Profile Checklist

  • Check that your business is eligible and verified.
  • Use your real trading name and accurate contact details.
  • Set your primary and relevant secondary categories.
  • Complete the available service information.
  • Keep your regular and special opening hours up to date.
  • Use the correct address or service-area settings.

3. Use Genuine Photos and Customer Reviews

Photos can help customers understand the type of business you operate. Tradespeople may show finished work, retailers can show products and businesses with customer-facing premises can display the space customers will visit.

Reviews provide another source of information for prospective customers. You can invite genuine customers to leave honest feedback and reply professionally to both positive and negative reviews.

Avoid purchasing reviews, publishing fabricated feedback or attempting to manipulate ratings. It is better to build an accurate record of customer experiences over time.

4. Connect Your Profile to a Helpful Website

A Google Business Profile should not have to explain everything about your business. Your website can provide more detailed service information, examples of work, answers to customer questions and clear enquiry options.

For example, a building contractor serving Anglesey may benefit from a dedicated plastering page, examples of completed projects and an explanation of the areas covered. A business covering several counties should describe that coverage accurately instead of creating dozens of repetitive pages with only the town name changed.

Your website should also work comfortably on mobile devices and make your phone number, email or enquiry form easy to find.

A Google Business Profile helps people discover your business. Your website helps them understand why they should choose it.

5. Review Performance and Keep Improving

Review the performance information available in your Google Business Profile. Consider whether relevant customers are reaching your website, contacting you or requesting directions where applicable.

Google's local ranking systems mainly consider relevance, distance and prominence. There is no way to buy or guarantee a better local ranking directly from Google.

Google Business Profile FAQs

Is Google Business Profile free in the UK?

Yes. Eligible businesses can create and manage a Google Business Profile for free. Professional agencies may charge for setup, optimisation or ongoing management services.

Can a service-area business in Anglesey have a Google Business Profile?

It may be eligible if it meets Google's business eligibility requirements. Businesses visiting customers should use the appropriate service-area and address visibility settings.

Does adding more towns improve Google Maps rankings?

Simply repeating town names does not guarantee better visibility. Google considers factors including relevance, distance and prominence. Describe genuine service areas accurately.
