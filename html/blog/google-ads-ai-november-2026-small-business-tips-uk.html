Google's official product announcements page lists a 5 November 2026 update called Optimize Holiday Ads with Google AI, Creative Drop #2. It highlights localisation of campaigns, festive product image creation in Asset Studio and the ability to turn photos into video ads for YouTube.

The same announcements list shows additional October Demand Gen updates focused on measurement and creative quality. New features can differ by account, campaign, country and rollout stage. Check availability in your own Google Ads account before planning a campaign around a specific tool.

Our recommendation is to see AI-generated creative as an optional production aid, not a substitute for authentic service information or well-chosen audiences.

Five useful Google Ads tips for builders and tradespeople

  1. Target service intent. Focus on searches for jobs you actually take on, such as loft conversions or plastering, rather than broad clicks about DIY advice.
  2. Define your real area. A builder serving Anglesey and Gwynedd should not pay for traffic from places they cannot reasonably serve.
  3. Use a dedicated landing page. Show relevant past work, service information and a short quote form.
  4. Make contact easy. A visible phone link and simple project enquiry process matter on mobile.
  5. Review qualified enquiries. Count useful quotation requests, not just clicks and form visits.

For example, a home extension enquiry might need project type, postcode, approximate timescale and a way to describe the property. An attractive advertisement is ineffective if the landing page gives no useful next step.

How tutors and education businesses can use paid advertising

Tuition businesses have different buying patterns. Parents may search for GCSE Maths tutoring, primary literacy support or local in-person Science tuition. The advert, landing page and enquiry form should all describe the same service.

Clear tutor information, age groups, lesson formats and pricing guidance where available can help visitors decide whether to enquire. Schools and education-related services should also review advertising policies and be careful with claims about student outcomes.

For businesses covering several areas, create relevant content for places that are genuinely served rather than copying identical adverts for every town.

When AI-generated marketing images make sense

AI tools may help prepare seasonal visuals, alternative layouts or product-style imagery. For a genuine product sold online, be careful not to misrepresent the item, its appearance or the customer experience. Authentic photos are often particularly valuable for construction work, tutoring teams, customer-facing premises and real projects.

Review every generated asset for brand consistency, factual accuracy and platform policy compliance. A convincing picture cannot replace a good offer, proof of work or clear service information.

Connect ads to a sales funnel rather than an unattended inbox

Visitors often need a second step: request a quotation, choose an appointment time, ask about lessons or buy a product. An ad may generate interest, but a slow response can waste that opportunity.

A sensible sales funnel and follow-up workflow can acknowledge enquiries, organise them by service, assign responsibility and prompt your team when further contact is needed. GoHighLevel CRM support is one option where it matches how the business operates.

How should a small business measure Google Ads?

Agree the intended outcome before launch: relevant form submissions, booked consultations, calls or completed orders. Ensure tracking is configured appropriately and review whether leads come from the target region.

Costs and results vary substantially by industry, season and location. Avoid promises that a fixed ad spend will produce a particular number of customers. Start with a manageable budget, learn what visitors do after clicking and refine the campaign from real evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI automatically create a successful Google Ads campaign?

AI can assist with campaign assets and optimisation, but advertisers still need accurate service details, good landing pages, appropriate targeting, policy compliance and meaningful measurement.

Are the November 2026 Google Ads AI features available to every UK advertiser?

Availability may vary by product, account, region and rollout. Check the latest Google Ads interface and official release information before relying on a particular feature.

Should a tradesperson advertise across all of Wales?

Only if the business can actually serve customers across that area. Builders in Anglesey, Bangor or Gwynedd may benefit from more precise location and service targeting.
