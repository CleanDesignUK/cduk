FAQs

Questions about sales funnels & landing pages.

Useful answers before you decide whether this service fits your business.

What is the difference between a website and a sales funnel?

A website supports broader discovery and multiple journeys. A funnel is usually narrower, guiding a specific audience toward one primary action such as a booking, application or enquiry.

Can you connect forms to a CRM?

Yes. We can connect lead capture to CRM pipelines and follow-up workflows depending on the platform and integration requirements.

Do you build landing pages for Google or Meta ads?

Yes. Campaign landing pages can be designed around a specific audience, message and conversion action with tracking in mind.
